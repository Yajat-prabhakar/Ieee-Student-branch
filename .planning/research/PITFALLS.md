# Pitfalls Research: IEEE Student Branch Portfolio Website

## Pitfall 1: Brand mismatch with IEEE expectations

- Warning signs: inconsistent typography, weak visual hierarchy, non-professional colors
- Prevention: define design tokens early and approve visual references before page build
- Phase mapping: Phase 1 and Phase 2

## Pitfall 2: Outdated leadership/events content

- Warning signs: old office-bearer names, missing recent events, no year labels
- Prevention: content schema with mandatory date/tenure fields and update checklist
- Phase mapping: Phase 3 and Phase 4

## Pitfall 3: Media-heavy pages causing slow loads

- Warning signs: large uncompressed galleries, high LCP on mobile
- Prevention: image optimization pipeline, strict size caps, lazy loading
- Phase mapping: Phase 4 and Phase 6

## Pitfall 4: Weak information architecture

- Warning signs: users cannot find council/society/contact pages in <=2 clicks
- Prevention: fixed top-level nav and clear breadcrumb/page headings
- Phase mapping: Phase 2 and Phase 6

## Pitfall 5: Non-actionable contact/feedback channel

- Warning signs: broken forms, no confirmation state, submissions lost
- Prevention: tested form endpoint with success/failure UX and backup email display
- Phase mapping: Phase 5 and Phase 6
